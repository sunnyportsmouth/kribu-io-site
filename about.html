About Kribu

Building toward a more intelligent operating layer.

Kribu comes from Sanskrit kṛtrima buddhi — artificial intellect.

Kribu is a company built around a simple belief: the next generation of software should not just record activity. It should understand context, reduce coordination overhead, and help people and organizations operate more effectively across time.

Our point of view

Intelligence alone is not enough. The systems that matter will be the ones that help people stay coordinated, maintain continuity, and move forward with less friction.

What Kribu is focused on

Personal AI systems

Helping individuals and households stay on top of reminders, follow-through, coordination, and the invisible work of everyday life.

Operational continuity systems

Helping organizations maintain continuity of execution when key people, responsibilities, and workflows are in transition.

AI-enabled modernization

Helping enterprises modernize legacy software and core architectures using practical AI-assisted workflows that speed up execution.

How we think

We prefer small wedges over oversized promises. We are interested in systems that become more valuable as context, responsibility, and complexity increase. We care about products and services that create meaningful leverage without making life or work feel heavier.
