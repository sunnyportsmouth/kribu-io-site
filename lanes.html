Kribu Lanes

Three connected areas of focus.

Kribu is operating across three lanes that share one underlying belief: intelligent systems should reduce friction, preserve continuity, and help people and organizations operate with more leverage.

The lanes

Lane One

Personal AI systems

Support for reminders, follow-through, coordination, and the invisible operational work of daily life.

Lane Two

Operational continuity systems

Systems that help organizations maintain continuity of execution when key people, obligations, and workflows shift.

Lane Three

AI-enabled modernization

Modernization work that helps enterprises upgrade legacy systems, evolve core architectures, and execute transformation faster.

Why they fit together

Each lane is fundamentally about coordination. In one case, that coordination lives in everyday life. In another, it lives in business continuity. In the third, it lives in the architecture and workflows of enterprise systems. Kribu is interested in the intelligent layer beneath all three.
